The library before the exhibition

The case briefing

At 3:30 p.m., just before the school’s history exhibition opened, Mrs Carter discovered that a rare illustrated book was missing from the main display. Four students had been near the library that afternoon, but nobody saw anyone leave with the book.

The detective must read the story, examine the clues, question the suspects and write a conclusion in English.

Past simple Past perfect Hypothesis

Your mission

  • 1
    Read the situation
    Understand what happened and when.
  • 2
    Examine the evidence
    Tap each clue on the board.
  • 3
    Interview suspects
    Compare their statements and alibis.
  • 4
    Write your theory
    Use evidence to justify your answer.
Teacher-friendly idea: pupils can first discuss in pairs, then write an individual theory in English.
